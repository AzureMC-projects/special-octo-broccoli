BUILD WITHOUT ANDROID STUDIO (GitHub, free):
1. Make a free account at github.com and create a new repository (private is fine).
2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in the CONTENTS of this folder,
   including the hidden .github folder. Put your image at
   app/src/main/assets/lockandhome.png first.
3. Open the repo's Actions tab, wait for "Build APK" to finish (~3-5 min).
4. Click the finished run, download "LockAndHome-apk" at the bottom, unzip it.
5. Send app-debug.apk to your phone, tap it, and allow "install unknown apps".
6. Open the app, allow notifications, and set battery usage to Unrestricted.
